Help Section — How to Use This System

1. Logging In

Enter your staff username and password on the login page. Only registered staff accounts can access the system.

2. Registering a New Appointment

From the Main Menu, choose "Register New Appointment". Fill in the patient's name, address, contact number, select a dentist and treatment type, then pick a date and time. Submit the form — a unique appointment number will be generated automatically and displayed on screen. Write this number down or give it to the patient, as it is used to look up the appointment later.

3. Finding an Appointment

Choose "Display Appointment Details" and type in the appointment number. All stored details for that visit will be shown.

4. Generating a Bill

Choose "Calculate & Print Bill", enter the appointment number, and click "Generate Bill". The system automatically calculates the consultation fee plus treatment cost (with a surcharge added automatically for emergency appointments). Click "Print Bill" to print a physical receipt for the patient.

5. Daily Reports

Choose "Daily Reports", pick a date, and view the full appointment schedule plus total revenue for that day.

6. Exiting the System

Click "Exit System (Log Out)" on the Main Menu, or "Log Out" in the top-right corner, to safely end your session.

Troubleshooting

  • If a form shows a red error message, check that every required field is filled in correctly (e.g. contact numbers must only contain digits and standard symbols).
  • If you are redirected back to the login page unexpectedly, your session may have expired — simply log in again.
  • If an appointment number is "not found", double-check it was typed exactly as generated (e.g. APT1001).
